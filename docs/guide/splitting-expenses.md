# Splitting expenses

Paid the whole dinner bill, or the rent for the flat? Split it so your budget only counts your share.

## Split an expense

Open the expense and add each person and how much they owe you. The form shows what others owe you
and **your share**.

## Owed to you

At the top of **Transactions**, **Owed to you** lists who still has to pay you back, per person and
per expense.

## When they pay you back

In **Owed to you**, tap **Paid** next to their share. Or, if their payment is already in your
transactions (an imported Venmo deposit, say), use that instead so it isn't counted twice.

A payback isn't counted as income: it takes away from
spending **in that category, in the month of the expense**. So if you paid $90 for dinner and two
friends paid you $30 each, your Eating out spending for that month is $30.

## Forgetting a debt

If someone's never going to pay, **Forget** it. It asks first, and **Undo** brings it back for a few
seconds afterwards.
