# Questions and problems

## Transactions

**A transaction I added isn't in the list.**
Your filters are remembered. Tap **Clear filters** (or **Clear search and filters**) at the top of
Transactions.

**I imported a file and some rows were left out.**
The review step lists what's **Left out** and why. Credit card payments are left out on purpose:
counting the payment and the purchases would count your spending twice. Rows you already have show
as **Already saved**.

**All my imported spending shows as income (or the other way round).**
Import again and, when matching columns, switch between **Negative (-12.50)** and
**Positive (12.50)**. Banks usually show spending as negative; some credit cards show it as positive.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
Tap **Undo** in the message that appears for a few seconds afterwards.

## Linked banks

**My card is paid off but the Dashboard still shows money owed.**
The card company only lowers its balance once a payment posts, often 1 to 3 business days later.
If the payment shows as processing on the card, it's already taken off **Owed on cards**. If not,
wait for it to post. Balances update every morning, or tap **Sync now** in **Settings → Linked banks**.

**It says transactions are still loading.**
Normal for a few minutes after linking. Tap **Sync now** later.

**A bank says it needs linking again.**
The bank ended access, for example after a password change, or your SimpleFIN subscription lapsed.
Unlink it and link it again.

**A new purchase hasn't appeared.**
SimpleFIN usually refreshes about once a day, so allow a day. Pending purchases appear in their own
section until they post.

**The bank's sign-in window opens and closes straight away.**
Allow pop-ups for the app's site. Some banks open their sign-in in a pop-up.

**It says the SimpleFIN setup token was already used.**
Each token works once. In SimpleFIN Bridge, disable it (if it wasn't you who used it) and create a new
one.

**Linking says two-factor sign-in is needed.**
Turn it on first in **Settings → Two-factor sign-in**.

## Signing in

**I forgot my password.**
Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the email.

**I lost the phone with my authenticator.**
Use your backup authenticator if you added one. If you didn't, ask the app's owner to help you get
back in, then [add a backup](./privacy-and-security.md#add-a-backup-authenticator).

## The app

**I'm not seeing the latest version.**
Open **Settings → About this app → Check for updates**, or reload the page.

**Still stuck?** Post in the suggestions forum (account menu, top right).
