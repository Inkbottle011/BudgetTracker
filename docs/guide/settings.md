# Settings and more

Settings is in order of importance: **Two-factor sign-in**, **Linked banks** and **Privacy mode**
first, then how the app looks, then the forum and **About this app**.

## Appearance

Five color themes, **Night sky** (the default), **Ocean**, **Forest**, **Sunset** and **Classic**,
each in light or dark. Money colors mean the same in all of them:

- <span style={{color: 'var(--bt-good)', fontWeight: 600}}>green</span> for money in and under budget
- <span style={{color: 'var(--bt-bad)', fontWeight: 600}}>red</span> for money out and over budget
- <span style={{color: 'var(--bt-saved)', fontWeight: 600}}>violet</span> for savings
- amber for getting close to a limit
- grey for transfers between your own accounts

## Moving background

An optional starry sky behind every page: **Stars**, **Warp**, **Nebula** or **None**. Choose its
speed, how many stars, its color, and whether to add shooting stars and planets. It holds still if
your device is set to reduce motion. It's saved on each device.

## Updates

**Settings → About this app** shows the version you're running, and **Check for updates** looks for a
newer one. When a new version is published, a bar at the top of the app offers to **Reload**.

## Suggestions forum

Post ideas and problems, upvote the ones you'd like, and reply. Open it from the account menu (your
initial, top right) or **Settings → Open the forum**.

- Everyone appears anonymously, as "Member 4F3A".
- Edit or delete your own posts and replies (with **Undo**).
- The developer marks posts **Planned**, **Done** or **Not planned**, and their replies are marked
  **Developer**.
