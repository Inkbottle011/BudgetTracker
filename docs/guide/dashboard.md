# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the summary of one period: a **day**, **week** (Monday to Sunday), **month** or
**year**. Pick the period at the top and use the arrows to step back and forward.

## Spending against your plan

The top panel shows what you've spent compared with what you planned for the period. A marker shows
where you'd be if you spent evenly through it, so you can tell at a glance whether you're ahead or
behind. Beside it are what you **earned**, **saved** and have **left**.

Charges your bank hasn't posted yet aren't counted. If there are any, a line underneath says
"Plus $12.50 pending at your bank, not counted until it posts."

## Budget bars

One bar per spending category, colored by how you're doing:

| Color | Means |
|---|---|
| Your theme's color | On track |
| Amber | Getting close: 85% of the plan or more |
| Red | Over |

Tap a bar to open that category's transactions for the period shown. Categories you spent in
without a budget are listed too.

## Balances from your banks

With a bank linked, a row shows **Checking**, **Savings** and **Owed on cards**. Tap one to see its
accounts. For cards, it also shows how much of the limit you're using, and warns from 30%, the point
where it starts to affect credit scores.

Paid off a card but the payment is still processing? It's already taken off **Owed on cards**, with
"$500.00 payment processing" underneath. Tap the tile and the card shows what your bank still
reports until the payment posts. See [Linking a bank](./linking-a-bank.md#paying-off-a-card).

The line underneath says when the balances were last updated. They refresh every morning, or when
you tap **Sync now** in Settings.

## Also on the Dashboard

- **Monthly trend**: spending, income and savings month by month
- **Coming up**: subscription charges due soon
- **Recent**: the latest transactions, with pending ones first, marked **Pending**
