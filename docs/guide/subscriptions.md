# Subscriptions

Anything that repeats, money out or in: rent, phone, streaming, gym, a regular paycheck. Totals per
month and per year are shown at the top.

## Add one

**Subscriptions → Add subscription**: the name, amount, how often, and when it starts (and ends, if
it does). Each charge is then added to your transactions **once, on its real date**. A monthly charge
on the 31st doesn't drift, and nothing is added twice.

**Pause** stops new charges until you **Resume**.

## Spotted for you (Looks like a subscription)

With a bank linked, the app looks for charges that repeat on a schedule:

- well-known services (Netflix, Spotify and the like) from the very first charge
- bank wording such as "recurring"
- two identical charges about a month apart

Each appears under **Looks like a subscription**. Tap **Add** to make it a subscription, or
**Not a subscription** to dismiss it for good.

## Price changes

When your bank charges a different amount than the subscription says, you'll be offered to update it
in one tap. The bank's real charge replaces the app's entry, so it isn't counted twice.
