# Getting started

Budget Tracker helps you plan a budget, keep track of every transaction, and see how the day, week,
month or year is going against your plan. It runs in your web browser at
**[managemymulah.netlify.app](https://managemymulah.netlify.app)**, so there's nothing to install.

## 1. Create your account

1. Open the app and choose **Create account**.
2. Enter your email and a password, then confirm your email from the message you're sent.
3. Sign in.

Forgot your password later? Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the email.

## 2. Get your transactions in

Pick whichever suits you. You can mix them later.

| Way | Best for | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Link your bank** | Hands-off: new transactions arrive every morning | [Linking a bank](./linking-a-bank.md) |
| **Import a file** | Bringing in history from your bank's website | **Transactions → Import CSV** ([how](./transactions.md#import-a-file-from-your-bank)) |
| **Type them in** | Cash, or if you'd rather not connect a bank | **Transactions → + Add** ([how](./transactions.md#add-a-transaction)) |

## 3. Plan your budget

Open **Budget → Plan with questions**. It asks how you're paid, what you pay for regularly and what
you're saving for, then works out how much to plan for each category every month. You can change
any amount before adding it. See [Budget](./budget.md).

## 4. Add your subscriptions

Rent, phone, streaming: anything that repeats. Add each one once under **Subscriptions** and its
charges appear on their dates. If you link a bank, repeating charges are spotted for you. See
[Subscriptions](./subscriptions.md).

## 5. Turn on two-factor sign-in

**Settings → Two-factor sign-in** adds a code from your phone when you sign in. It keeps your account
safe, and you need it before you can link a bank. See
[Privacy and security](./privacy-and-security.md#two-factor-sign-in).

## Finding your way around

The tabs along the bottom (or the side, on a computer) are:

- **Dashboard**: how the period is going, your balances and recent activity
- **Transactions**: everything you've spent, earned and saved
- **Budget**: your plan for the year
- **Subscriptions**: things that repeat
- **Settings**: security, linked banks, privacy and looks

Your initial in the top right opens the account menu (the suggestions forum, and signing out). The
eye next to it turns on [privacy mode](./privacy-and-security.md#privacy-mode).
