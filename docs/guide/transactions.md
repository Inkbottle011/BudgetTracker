# Transactions

Everything you've spent, earned and saved, newest first.

## Types

| Type | What it is | Counts as |
|---|---|---|
| **Expense** | Money you spent | Spending |
| **Income** | Pay and other money in | Income |
| **Savings** | Money put aside | Saved |
| **Investment** | Money invested | Saved |
| **Reimbursement** | Refunds and paybacks | Takes away from spending in its category |
| **Transfer** | Money moving between your own accounts | Neither (shown, never counted) |
| **Withdrawal** | Money taken out of savings | Lowers your savings |

You choose the first four. Linked banks also use the last three.

## Add a transaction

**Transactions → + Add**: enter the date, amount, type, category and a name, then **Save**. To make a
copy of an existing one (handy for something you buy often), open it and tap **Duplicate**.

## Import a file from your bank

Most banks let you download your transactions as a **CSV** file from their website.

1. **Transactions → Import CSV → Choose a CSV file**.
2. **Match the columns**: the app guesses which column is the date, amount, description and so on.
   Check its guesses. If your bank shows spending as positive numbers (some credit cards do), pick
   **Positive (12.50)**.
3. **Review** what's **Ready to import**, what's **Already saved** (so you never get doubles) and
   what's **Left out** (like credit card payments, which would count your spending twice).
4. Import.

Categories are suggested from what you've chosen before.

## Sort out uncategorized ones

When transactions have no category, a panel groups them **by place**, so different spellings of the
same store sit together, with a suggested category for each. Choose one and every transaction in
that group gets it.

## Rename a place

Bank descriptions can be messy ("SQ *BLUE BOTTLE #12"). Rename it once ("Blue Bottle") and future
transactions from that place use your name too.

## Split an expense

Paid for something with friends? Open the expense and add who owes you what. **Owed to you** at the
top of the page tracks who still has to pay you back. See [Splitting expenses](./splitting-expenses.md).

## Find things

- **Search** by name or note.
- **Filter** by type, category (or uncategorized) and dates: this month, last month, the last 30
  days, this year, or any range. What's shown and its totals appear at the top.
- **Sort** by date, type or amount: tap a column heading.
- Your filters and sort are remembered on this device. Can't find something? Tap **Clear filters**.
- **Export CSV** downloads what's shown.

## Pending charges

With a bank linked, charges it hasn't posted yet appear in their own section. They aren't counted
until they post, then they become ordinary transactions.

## Deleting

Deleting asks first, and an **Undo** button appears for a few seconds afterwards, which puts
everything back as it was, including who owed you for it.
