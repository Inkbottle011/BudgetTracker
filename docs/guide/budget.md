# Budget

Your plan for the year: what you expect to earn, spend in each category, and save.

## Plan with questions

The easiest way to start. **Budget → Plan with questions** walks you through four steps:

1. **Your income**: how many jobs you have and how you're paid: an hourly rate and hours a week, a
   salary, or an amount per paycheck, less taxes.
2. **What you pay for regularly**: weekly, monthly or yearly. If you already have transactions,
   what you've really spent lately is shown as a hint.
3. **What you're saving for**: a target by a date, a set amount each month, or a share of your pay.
4. **Check it**: the monthly amounts it worked out. Change any of them, then add them to your budget
   from this month on or for the whole year.

## Two ways to see it

- **Simple**: each item once, with its monthly amount. Change it for every month in one tap.
  Items that differ from month to month say so.
- **By month**: every month side by side on a computer (one month at a time on a phone), with
  **fill right**, totals, and what's left over each month.

## Changing your categories

- **Rename**: renaming a budget item renames its transactions and subscriptions too.
- **Combine**: rename it to a category you already have and the two become one, with their planned
  amounts added together.
- **Split in two**: for example Food into Groceries and Eating out. Past purchases are sorted place
  by place, with a guess for each that you can change. The budget is shared out by what you really
  spend in each, and new purchases follow.
- **Categories not in your budget**: categories your transactions use but your budget doesn't (like
  a typo) are listed so you can move them to another category or add them.
- **Reorder** items within a section.

## A new year

**Copy last year's budget** fills an empty year with last year's plan, ready to adjust.

## Deleting

Deleting a budget item asks first, and **Undo** puts it back for a few seconds afterwards.
